# QTLab Research Methodology

Every piece of research follows six stages.

1. Observation
2. Research Question
3. Hypothesis
4. Experiment
5. Evidence
6. Conclusion

Research may never skip a stage.

Experiments exist to challenge hypotheses, not confirm them.

Evidence accumulates across experiments.

Conclusions remain provisional until new evidence appears.
