# Philosophy

QTLab is not designed to maximize profits.

QTLab is designed to maximize confidence in research.

Its purpose is to help researchers reject weak hypotheses before risking real capital.

The platform values:

- reproducibility
- statistical rigor
- transparency
- modularity
- scientific methodology

over:

- optimization
- curve fitting
- black-box automation

A successful backtest is not considered evidence that a strategy works.

It is merely one piece of evidence requiring further validation.
