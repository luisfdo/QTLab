# Generation 1 Capability Map

## Capability 1
Capture Curiosity

### Goal

Allow a researcher to begin a Study from an observation.

### Produces

- Observation
- Initial reasoning

---

## Capability 2
Formulate Research

### Goal

Transform an observation into a testable question.

### Produces

- Research Question
- Hypothesis
- Counter-Hypothesis (optional in Gen 1, required later)

---

## Capability 3
Design the Investigation

### Goal

Describe how the hypothesis will be tested.

### Produces

- Methodology
- Dataset selection
- Parameters
- Assumptions

---

## Capability 4
Conduct Experiment

### Goal

Execute the methodology.

### Produces

- Raw results
- Metrics
- Charts
- Experiment Report

---

## Capability 5
Evaluate Evidence

### Goal

Transform results into evidence.

### Produces

- Evidence Package
- Supporting observations
- Contradictory observations

---

## Capability 6
Assess Findings

### Goal

Allow the researcher to record their current understanding.

### Produces

- Assessment
- Confidence level
- Remaining uncertainties

---

## Capability 7
Reflect

### Goal

Capture what the researcher learned.

### Produces

- Reflection
- Lessons learned
- New questions

---

## Capability 8
Preserve Knowledge

### Goal

Ensure the Study remains reproducible.

### Produces

- Complete Study
- Timeline
- Reproducible artifact

---

**This is no longer software.**

**It's a workflow.**
