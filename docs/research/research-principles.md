# Research Principles

1. *Curiosity precedes conviction.*

    Every experiment begins with a question, not an answer.

3. *Evidence outweighs intuition.*

    Strong opinions must yield to reproducible evidence.

3. *Every hypothesis deserves an attempt to be disproven.*

    Research is not about proving ourselves right.

4. *Negative results are valuable.*

    Rejected hypotheses prevent wasted effort and improve future research.

5. *Knowledge compounds.*

    Every experiment contributes to organizational understanding.

6. *Research is iterative.*

    Every conclusion should inspire a better question.
